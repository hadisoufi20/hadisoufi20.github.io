SSRN Working Paper · posted 21 September 2026

A Three-Layer Reference Architecture for Deterministic Control in Agentic Trading Systems

Hadi Soufi

Abstract

This paper formalizes a three-layer reference architecture for agentic trading systems: a discovery layer, a reasoning layer, and a deterministic execution layer with a hard-gated kill-switch. It defines responsibilities, interfaces, failure boundaries, and evaluation criteria, framing the split as a testable specification rather than a design metaphor.
