SSRN Working Paper · 2026

From Backtest to Deployment: A Staged-Capital Protocol for Evaluating Agentic Trading Systems

Hadi Soufi

Abstract

This paper proposes a staged-capital evaluation protocol for agentic trading systems: zero-weight shadow validation, forward paper-testing on live data, minimal live capital gated by deterministic mechanical exits, and gradual scaling gated by pre-registered statistical tests. The protocol is designed as a deployable and falsifiable engineering workflow rather than a reporting convention.
