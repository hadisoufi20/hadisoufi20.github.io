Books

Seven technical books.

AI, blockchain, and quantitative trading. Available on Amazon.

  • The Proprietary Agent

    A blueprint for a private multi-agent trading system built around three roles: a Scout that gathers information, a Contextualizer that reasons over it, and a Guardian that controls risk and execution. Nine chapters cover orchestration, privately hosted fine-tuned models, retrieval and memory, and a staged deployment protocol from shadow mode to gradual autonomy.

    Amazon
  • The Velocity Paradox

    Argues that human reaction time has become a structural disadvantage in markets where algorithms act in microseconds. Eleven chapters cover decision latency and its costs, order-flow engineering, stress testing beyond backtests, asymmetric payoffs, adversarial algorithms, and a roadmap for moving from discretionary decision-making to system design.

    Amazon
  • The Market ArchitectThe Systemic Logic of Global Finance and AI Decision-Engineering

    A reference to financial markets in nineteen chapters: forex, equities, commodities, fixed income and crypto; brokers and execution; risk management and position sizing; strategy design, backtesting and journaling; derivatives, liquidity and order flow; portfolio allocation; AI in analysis and execution; trading psychology; and the legal and fraud landscape.

    Amazon
  • The Trading CopilotEngineering the Human-AI Edge without Coding

    A no-code guide to working with AI as a trading copilot. Eight chapters cover the division of roles between trader and model, prompt engineering and context injection for financial decisions, strategy stress-testing, custom monitoring agents, real-time bias filtering, and AI-assisted performance review. Its premise: AI is a copilot, not the pilot.

    Amazon
  • The Blockchain BlueprintVolume 1: The Genesis

    Blockchain for first-time readers: what it is, how it works, how it is secured, where it applies beyond cryptocurrencies, and the opportunities and challenges ahead.

    Amazon
  • The Blockchain BlueprintVolume 2: The Infrastructure

    A practical guide for active users: wallets, exchanges, a first transaction, smart contracts, NFTs and decentralized applications, DeFi, and how to avoid common risks and scams.

    Amazon
  • The Blockchain BlueprintVolume 3: The Frontier

    The systems view for specialists: advanced architecture and security, oracles and off-chain data, DAOs and decentralized governance, Web3 and the metaverse, advanced DeFi, and open challenges.

    Amazon
